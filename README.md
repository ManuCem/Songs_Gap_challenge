# 🎵 Lyrics Gap Challenge

A free web app for learning English with music: search any song, pick a level, and fill in the missing lyrics while the song plays (no pausing to rewind!), then play it again to beat your score, with a last chance of three 20-second rewinds before the final results.
Correct words turn green on their own, hints are counted, and with a Google account your games, tries, XP, streaks, saved songs with labels and per-song leaderboards are kept privately in your stats.
It runs entirely in the browser (GitHub Pages + Firebase + free lyrics and music search) and can be installed on your phone like an app.
